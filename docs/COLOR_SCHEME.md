Ceramic & Clay
Concept: This scheme draws from the world of handmade pottery and ceramics. It’s earthy, tactile, and grounded in the natural world. It speaks to the process of shaping something raw into something both beautiful and functional. The colors are warm, human, and inviting, reflecting the personal "soul" of your projects.
Feel: Warm, touchy, organic, and honest.
Palette:
Unglazed Bisque (Background): #FAF6F0 — A light, textured beige that feels warm and natural.
Earthy Brown (Text): #4E342E — A deep, rich brown that is warm and highly readable.
Terracotta (Primary Accent): #E07A5F — A muted, sun-baked orange-red, perfect for bringing warmth and personality to headers and tags.
Sage Glaze (Secondary Accent): #81B29A — A soft, calming green, like a ceramic glaze. Ideal for secondary information or to categorize certain project types like "Utilities".
Sandstone (Highlight Accent): #F2CC8F — A gentle, sandy yellow for subtle highlights and hover states.
